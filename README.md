# **3.2 Situational Leadership (Hersey & Blanchard)**

Situational Leadership states that effective leaders adapt their style depending on the competence and commitment of the individual they are supporting.

The four styles are:

| Style | Description | When I Used It |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **Directing** | High direction, low support | When giving clear instructions or clarifying expectations |
| **Coaching** | High direction, high support | When helping a colleague develop skills or confidence |
| **Supporting** | Low direction, high support | When a colleague was capable but lacked confidence |
| **Delegating** | Low direction, low support | When a colleague was competent and motivated |

### **How I applied it**
Across the synoptic, I adapted my style depending on the situation:

- In meetings, I used a **supporting** style to encourage contributions.  
- In coaching, I used a **coaching** style to guide reflection and development.  
- In difficult conversations, I used a **directing** style to set clear expectations.
